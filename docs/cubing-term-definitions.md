| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Trigger | A short sequence of moves able to be performed fast. They generally 3 or 4 moves. Typically, a trigger is an R or L move, followed by a U move (U, U' or U2), and ending with the reverse of first move. For example R U R'. |
| AUF | Adjust U face. Means a U move (U, U', or U2) was applied to the start or end of an alg. |
| Rotation | Any x y or z move. For example, z2. Larger suffixes like x3, y3', etc. are not valid. |
| Move | Any turn of the face or faces of the cube, including full cube rotations (x, y, z). |
| Angle | The direction the cube is viewed at by the cuber. Changing the viewing angle fundamentally changes the "location" of a piece. x y and z moves change the angle of the cube. Sometimes, angle refers to a combination of a rotation and an AUF applied to a cube. |
